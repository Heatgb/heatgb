Boiler Servicing & Repairs

Boiler Servicing

Having your boiler serviced regularly will ensure that it remains safe and runs efficiently, reducing the risk of a breakdown and prolonging the life of your boiler. Boiler manufacturers recommend that their appliances be serviced annually, and if your boiler is under warranty, you need to have an annual service to keep your warranty valid.

A boiler service should only be carried out by a qualified and registered heating engineer.

What to expect from your Heat GB boiler service:

  • Visual check to ensure your boiler still meets current standards
  • Boiler fired to identify any working faults
  • Boiler casing removed to check all main components (burner, heat exchanger, main injector, spark/sensor probe)
  • Landlord Gas Safety devices checked to ensure correct operation
  • Flue terminals checked — unobstructed and internal flue components sealed properly
  • Gas valve adjusted to check efficient and proper combustion (using a flue gas analyser)
  • Inside casing surfaces cleaned
  • Boiler parts cleaned if necessary
  • Gas tightness test conducted to ensure no leaks
  • Boiler casing replaced and sealed properly

Which? magazine recommends that a boiler service should take a minimum of 30 minutes.

Landlord Gas Safety (CP12)

A Landlords Gas Safety Inspection Certificate (CP12) is required by law to be issued every 12 months by a Gas Safe Registered Engineer. It is a legal document which confirms that all gas appliances and installations work lawfully and within normal parameters at the time of inspection.

We can carry out these inspections on behalf of landlords and letting agents, as well as ensuring that all gas appliances are regularly maintained and kept in good working order.

Need to book your annual CP12? Our experienced staff are always happy to help.

Boiler & Heating Repairs

If you have a temperamental heating system — it makes noises, you have hot water in the header tank, cold radiators, or need to continually bleed them — then our experts can help. It doesn't necessarily mean a whole new system is required; there are many reasons for any central heating system to degrade and we will advise on the best way to get your heating back to its maximum potential quickly.

Our engineers have previously worked directly for boiler manufacturers and have an in-depth knowledge of faults that occur with a range of boilers. No matter what type of boiler your household has, our team of experts can quickly diagnose and repair your boiler where viable, using quality components and parts.

All repair work is covered with a 30-day labour guarantee and a 1-year parts guarantee.

Common Boiler Issues We Fix

  • No heating or hot water
  • Boiler making banging, whistling or gurgling noises
  • Pilot light keeps going out

  • Radiators cold at top or bottom
