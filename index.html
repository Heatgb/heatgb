02Powerflushing

Clear the sludge. Feel the difference.

A power flush gives your central heating a thorough internal clean, usually improving its performance — which can lead to lower gas bills and extend the lifespan of your boiler.

  • Lukewarm radiators
  • Poor hot water
  • Increased noise
  • Sludge build-up

We flush your system in different directions, using special chemicals to break down sludge, iron filings and debris, then remove it via inline magnetic filtration.

Safe, efficient, warranty-ready.

Manufacturers recommend an annual service — and if your boiler is under warranty, you need one to keep it valid. Every Heat GB service includes:

05Landlord gas safety (CP12)

Legally safe, every 12 months.

A Landlords Gas Safety Inspection Certificate (CP12) is required by law every 12 months, issued by a Gas Safe registered engineer. It confirms all gas appliances and installations work lawfully and within normal parameters at the time of inspection.

We carry out inspections on behalf of landlords and letting agents, and keep your appliances regularly maintained and in good working order.

04 — Gas safety

Gas safety isn’t optional.

All of our engineers are on the Gas Safe Register and will show their official identity card when you ask to see it. Only use a Gas Safe registered engineer to fit, fix and service your gas appliances.

Gas Safe Register is the official gas safety organisation in Great Britain. It replaced CORGI on 1st April 2009, and membership is mandatory for any company or engineer working with gas appliances. To find out more call 0800 408 5500 or visit the Gas Safe Register website.
