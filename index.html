We are Gas Safe Registered

Heat GB takes gas safety seriously. All of our engineers are on the Gas Safe Register and will have their official identity card when you ask to see it. Gas Safe Register is the official gas safety organisation in Great Britain — replacing CORGI on 1st April 2009.

If you would like to find out more about Gas Safe Register you can call 0800 408 5500 or visit the Gas Safe Register website.
